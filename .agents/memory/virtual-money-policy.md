---
name: Virtual-money policy
description: Product rules for Naira Demo's starting balance and simulated financial activity.
---

New Naira Demo accounts start with ₦500,000,000 Virtual Balance / Demo Money. No real money, banks, cards, NIN collection, biometric verification, mobile money, or external payment gateways are involved; service flows are simulations.

**Why:** The user's latest balance requirement sets new accounts to ₦500,000,000 and keeps Naira Demo a safe fintech simulation.

**How to apply:** Keep the ₦500,000,000 grant consistent across account creation and welcome history. Label balances as virtual/demo money, restrict transfers to registered in-app wallets, and ensure service simulations never debit balances or call external providers.
