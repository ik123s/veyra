---
name: Virtual-money policy
description: Product rules for Naira Demo's starting balance and simulated financial activity.
---

New Naira Demo accounts start with ₦50,000 Virtual Balance / Demo Money. This supersedes the earlier ₦500,000,000 starting-balance figure. No real money, banks, cards, identity verification, mobile money, or external payment gateways are involved; service flows are simulations.

**Why:** The later Phase 3 product requirement explicitly replaced the earlier starting amount and limited the product to a safe fintech simulation.

**How to apply:** Keep the ₦50,000 grant consistent across account creation and welcome history. Label balances as virtual/demo money, restrict transfers to registered in-app wallets, and ensure service simulations never debit balances or call external providers.
